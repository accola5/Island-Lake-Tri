Getting there & packet pickup

Island Lake County Park sits just off County Highway D, a few minutes from downtown Barnes. Packet pickup is available Friday evening at the park pavilion and again race-morning starting at 6:15 AM. Parking is on the grass field north of the boat landing — follow the marked signs in from the entrance road.

Swim

A counter-clockwise loop off the main beach, hugging the shoreline for sighting. Wetsuits are welcome — Island Lake typically runs cool through late September. Kayak support is stationed every 100 meters.

Course notes

EntryBeach start, in-water wave deployment
SightingOrange buoys at each turn, yellow on the final stretch
ExitBoat landing ramp, carpeted for bare feet

Bike

Rolling out from transition onto quiet forest-service roads, this leg winds through pine stands with a couple of short rises before looping back along the lake road. Roads are closed to through-traffic but not fully closed to residents — ride predictably.

Course notes

SurfacePaved county road, one short gravel connector
AidWater stop near the halfway turnaround
CautionDescent into the second curve — marshals posted

Run

Out of transition and onto shaded park trails before a lakeside out-and-back to the finish chute at the pavilion. Mostly packed dirt with a short paved stretch near the start.

Course notes

SurfacePacked singletrack and gravel park road
AidWater and electrolyte stop at the turnaround
FinishGrass chute into the pavilion, timing mats at the line
